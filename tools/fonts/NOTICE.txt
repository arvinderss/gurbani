Bundled Gurmukhi webfonts
=========================

The woff2 files in this directory are embedded (base64) into the reader's
single-file build by tools/make-fonts.js -> src/app/fonts.css. Only the
Gurmukhi unicode-range subset is embedded per weight.

The fonts are redistributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 as provided
by Google Fonts and are unchanged apart from Google's WOFF2 subsetting.

- Noto Sans Gurmukhi   (400, 700)  source: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+Gurmukhi
- Noto Serif Gurmukhi  (400, 700)  source: https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Serif+Gurmukhi
- Mukta Mahee          (400, 700)  source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Mukta+Mahee
